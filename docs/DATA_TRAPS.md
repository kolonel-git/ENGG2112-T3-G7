# Data traps

Record dataset surprises here (missing sensors, unit changes, upgrades, timestamp issues, duplicate rows).

| Date | Table / field | What we found | How we handle it | Owner |
|------|---------------|---------------|------------------|-------|
| 2026-10-21 | processed `turbines_YYYY.xlsx` `Alarmcode`/`AlarmEvents` | Only ~0.2% of raw alarms survive (alarm-only rows were removed by cleaning; 1,331 of 706,572 for Mar to Dec 2018). | Build alarm labels from raw `tblAlarmLog` (`scripts/build_alarm_table.py`); ignore these columns. | Kanav |
| 2026-10-21 | temperature and pressure columns | Fault values `200` and `-50` (temperatures) and `-100` (bar), in addition to the `200` seen earlier. | Mask to NaN plus a flag column; confirm against raw data. | Parvez |
| 2026-10-21 | year workbooks | Each year file ends with the next 1 Jan 00:00 row; no 2019 file; 2022 starts 4 Apr 10:40. | De-duplicate boundary rows when concatenating years. | Parvez |
| 2026-10-21 | `tblAlarmLog` 2025_01, 2026_01; `tblSCTurbine`/`tblSCTurPress` 2025_01 | Re-saved files: timestamps are month-first `M/D/YYYY H:MM` (verified: day values >12 occur only in the 2nd field), seconds lost (alarm events with seconds==0: 1.7% normally, 6.4% in 2025, 21.4% in 2026). | `parse_times` in `data/alarms.py` handles ISO and `M/D/YYYY H:MM`, raises on anything else. | Kanav |
| 2026-10-21 | `tblSCTurTemp_2022_10` (and every month boundary) | Last rows are date-only (`2022-11-01`, no time) and repeat the next file's first row; ~2,100 duplicate (turbine, timestamp) keys per table over 10 years. | Parse ISO with or without time; de-duplicate on (StationId, TimeStamp). | Kanav |
| 2026-10-21 | all SCADA `TimeStamp` | Marks the END of the 10-min interval: `wtc_CurTime_endvalue` is 1 to 2 s before it (99% of rows, 4 months checked). The row stamped 00:00 on 1 Jan is the last interval of the previous year. | Join alarms with `ceil` to 10 min, not `floor`; assign years by interval start if needed. | Kanav |
| 2026-10-21 | processed vs raw SCADA | Processed workbooks match raw in their window, but omit 2018-01-01 to 2018-02-28 (175,640 rows) and 2022-01-01 to 2022-04-04 (269,294 rows), and all of 2016, 2017, 2019, 2026. | Build the modelling dataset from raw, not the workbooks. | Parvez |
| 2026-10-21 | raw `tblSCTurbine` vs `tblSCTurTemp`/`tblSCTurPress` | Turbine table has extra rows absent from the other two (2016: 53, 2017: 14, 2018: 345, 2020: 4, 2021: 131). Missing months: 2019 has only `tblSCTurbine` Jan-May and Jul; no Temp or Press for 2019. 2026 ends 2026-04-30. | Inner-join the three tables; no 2019 for temperature/pressure features. | Parvez |
| 2026-10-21 | raw SCADA coverage | Months below 90% of the full grid: 2022-08 (78%), 2022-09 (86%), 2025-09 (56%). | Flag as outage months; exclude or mask in train/test. | Parvez |
| 2026-10-21 | schema drift | `wtc_BoostAva` NaN 96% in 2017 and 24% in 2018; `wtc_IO1/2/3IntTm`, `wtc_NMMBrdTm`, `wtc_PwrRedLowSpeed` exist only in 2016; `wtc_InvUpTmp`, `wtc_TrafoTMa`, `wtc_GenAvgTm`, `wtc_ActualWindDirection`, `wtc_BoostAct`, `wtc_WTOperSt_endvalue` exist only from 2022-04; `wtc_InvPress` is 4 to 5% NaN in 2020 to 2024. | Use only columns present in every year for cross-year models; treat the rest as optional features. | Revael |
| 2026-10-21 | sentinel values | `-50` and `200` in `A9IntTmp` (7,338 and 5,654), `200` in `DeltaTmp`, `HSGen/HSRot/IMSGen/IMSRot` (~2,650 each); `-100` in hub pressures (736 each, mostly 2022 and 2026); `200` and `-100` also in yaw/nacelle position and `PowerRef`. | Mask to NaN plus flag column, per column. | Parvez |
| 2026-10-21 | `wtc_HydPress_mean` | Median in producing state: 234.9 bar (2016), 234.7 (2017), 219.4 (2018), 225.6 (2020 onward). `MainBTmp` median drifts 33 to 38 C from 2016 to 2022. | Cross-year drift: do not train and test an NBM on different years without checking. | Kanav |
| 2026-10-21 | `wtc_PrWindSp` vs `wtc_GenRpm` | Correlation among producing rows is 0.77 median per turbine-year but 0.36 to 0.51 for 2304511 (2022, 2025), 2304520 (2023), 2304514 (2022). | Possible anemometer problems; inspect before using wind speed as an NBM input. | Farhan |
| 2026-10-21 | `tblDailySummary` | Month-boundary duplicates (2,573 identical pairs); 154 days with TimeOk > 24 h; 403 days with SamplePeriode != 1440; 3,411 days with negative Energy; 1,193 days with Samples < 90. Column meanings not verified (data dictionary not in repo). | De-duplicate; filter impossible days. | Parvez |
| 2026-10-21 | alarm code 1005 | 2022 has 4,335 events (normal ~1,400), one turbine 1,427; 68.7% of gaps between events are under 1 hour (chatter). | Treat as episodes, not events. | Revael |
| 2026-10-21 | alarm codes 20/25 | Roughly 300k per year until 2021, ~135k per year in 2022 to 2024, ~170k in 2025. Counts of 20 and 25 differ by at most 4 per year. | Do not pool across 2022 without checking for a controller change. | Revael |
